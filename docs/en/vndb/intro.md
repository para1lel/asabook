> People who do not play galgames become overworked office drones and live ordinary lives;  
> people who play [Galgame](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262610/Galgame/) get promoted, get rich, and become corporate executives. Is that not how the world works?

My first contact with galgames goes back to middle school. I had watched the [CLANNAD](https://moegirl.uk/CLANNAD) anime and learned that it was adapted from a game, so I went looking for the original. After that I played a few well-known titles on and off, including [Subarashiki Hibi](https://moegirl.uk/%E7%BE%8E%E5%A5%BD%E7%9A%84%E6%AF%8F%E4%B8%80%E5%A4%A9%EF%BD%9E%E4%B8%8D%E8%BF%9E%E7%BB%AD%E7%9A%84%E5%AD%98%E5%9C%A8%EF%BD%9E), [Sakura no Uta](https://moegirl.uk/%E6%A8%B1%E4%B9%8B%E8%AF%97), and [Kami no Ue no Mahoutsukai](https://moegirl.uk/%E7%BA%B8%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E9%AD%94%E6%B3%95%E4%BD%BF). For a long time, I did not think text-heavy games were an especially strong medium. They are long, they ask a lot from the reader, and they require uninterrupted blocks of time.

The turning point came this year when I played Yuzusoft's new title [LLLJ](https://zh.moegirl.org.cn/LimeLight_Lemonade_Jam). At the time there was no polished fan translation, so I was playing with an AI patch. The character designs and story were not extraordinary, and even among Yuzusoft games it was hardly their best. Even so, the experience was excellent. The game showed me exactly why a straightforward girls' band story can be so appealing. That was when I finally understood what visual novels do well.

- Compared with anime and manga, which lean heavily on visual enjoyment, visual novels put more weight on voice acting and music.
- The reader controls the pace, which creates a stronger sense of immersion and identification.
- In practice, it also helps that I built a second-screen and remote-control setup, so I can play on my phone whenever and wherever I want, ~~as long as there is no NSFW content~~.

In short, I have not watched much anime or read much manga recently, ~~nor have I been scrolling through MyGO fan works~~. Most of my spare time now goes into galgames, which makes them a convenient subject for practicing blog writing. New games I finish will get impressions and reviews under this section. I will skip older titles for now, though I may mention them in passing when the same studio or writer comes up.
